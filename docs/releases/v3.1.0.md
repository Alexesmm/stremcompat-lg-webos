# StremCompat 3.1.0

## New in the player's settings
- **Episodes:** the season's episodes, to jump to another one without leaving the player. It opens with the source that matches the one playing, like the next episode.
- **Chapters:** when the file has them, a list to jump to each one.
- **Files in this torrent:** when a torrent holds several films or episodes, the others can be played from here.
- **Picture:** the whole picture, or grown to fill the screen. The choice is kept.
- **Speed:** from 0.75× to 1.5×, where the TV allows it.

## Subtitles and audio
- Your languages come first in the subtitle list; the others are under **More languages**.
- The audio list starts with your language and shows each track's details, such as "Portuguese · Dolby Digital 5.1".
- Subtitles can be moved up or down and made more transparent, in **Style**.
- The subtitle delay moves in steps of 0.1 s, and faster while the key is held.

## Fixes
- **Autoplay next episode:** it now starts when the addon is slow, when the episode list arrives late, and when the TV never reports that the video ended. Two installations of the same addon are no longer mixed up.
- **Library button:** the plus sign appears once.
- **Sources that drop:** a direct or debrid link that stops while playing opens again where it was, once, before another source is offered.

## Storage
- With no room on the TV, torrents can use a USB drive, if the TV lets the app write on it.
- **Settings > Clear torrent cache** frees what torrents left behind.
- **Diagnostics** shows where torrents are kept and how much cache they use, and how fast the screen answers the remote.

## Other
- A picture left paused for five minutes goes dark, to protect OLED screens. Any key brings it back.
- After updating, the app says once what is new.

Choose `StremCompat-v3.1.0-1080p.ipk` for Full HD and 4K screens, or `StremCompat-v3.1.0-720p.ipk` for smaller screens.
