# StremCompat 4.0.0

## Highlights
- **Sign in with Stremio or Nuvio.** A Nuvio account (experimental) brings its profiles: choose **who is watching** after signing in, and change it in Settings. Each profile keeps its own addons, library and progress. Profiles protected with a PIN are not available yet. Signing out ends only this TV's session.
- **First start:** if you are signed in with Stremio, the app first tells you that a Nuvio account can be used now. Then two questions, both changeable later in Settings.
  - **Simple** shows only the essentials in Settings and in the player, with the best source first; **Complete** keeps every option.
  - **Standard** is the look of earlier versions. **Modern** is a large billboard in the style of the big streaming apps: full-width, sharper backgrounds, a bigger title with synopsis and cast, and larger rows; it is not recommended for webOS 3 or slower TVs. **Light** turns effects off and uses smaller images and less memory, for webOS 3 and TVs with little memory.
- **Home opens once, filled:** the opening screen stays until the first rows are in, instead of empty rows that fill in and blink.
- **Home rows:** **Because you watched…** from the genre of what you watched last, and the most popular catalog ranks its first ten.
- **Login screen:** the language opens as a list, and the account is a Stremio / Nuvio switch.

## New in the player's settings
- **Episodes:** the season's episodes, to jump to another one without leaving the player. It opens with the source that matches the one playing, like the next episode.
- **Chapters:** when the file has them, a list to jump to each one.
- **Files in this torrent:** when a torrent holds several films or episodes, the others can be played from here.
- **Picture:** the whole picture, or grown to fill the screen. The choice is kept.
- **Speed:** from 0.75× to 1.5×, where the TV allows it.

## Subtitles and audio
- Your languages come first in the subtitle list; the others are under **More languages**.
- The audio list starts with your language and shows each track's details, such as "Portuguese · Dolby Digital 5.1".
- Subtitles can be moved up or down and made more transparent, in **Style**.
- The subtitle delay moves in steps of 0.1 s, and faster while the key is held.
- **ASS subtitle styling** (Settings, off by default): ASS subtitles from addons keep their position on screen, italics, bold and colours. Fonts and effects are left out, since they are too heavy for these TVs.

## Fixes
- **Autoplay next episode:** it now starts when the addon is slow, when the episode list arrives late, and when the TV never reports that the video ended. Two installations of the same addon are no longer mixed up.
- **Library button:** the plus sign appears once.
- **Sources that drop:** a direct or debrid link that stops while playing opens again where it was, once, before another source is offered.

## Storage
- With no room on the TV, torrents can use a USB drive, if the TV lets the app write on it.
- **Settings > Clear torrent cache** frees what torrents left behind.
- **Diagnostics** shows where torrents are kept and how much cache they use, and how fast the screen answers the remote.

## Other
- **Latin American Spanish**, chosen by itself when the TV is set to Spanish outside Spain.
- **Settings > Test connection:** how long Stremio takes to answer, and how fast the last source downloads.
- Going back to a season returns to the episode it was left on.
- A long description on Home rolls up slowly instead of being cut off.
- When the app stopped while starting, the next start says at which step, for reports.
- A picture left paused for five minutes goes dark, to protect OLED screens. Any key brings it back.
- After updating, the app says once what is new.

Choose `StremCompat-v4.0.0-1080p.ipk` for Full HD and 4K screens, or `StremCompat-v4.0.0-720p.ipk` for smaller screens.
