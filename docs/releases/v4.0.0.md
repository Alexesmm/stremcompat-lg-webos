# StremCompat 4.0.0

Everything since 3.0.6.

> **You will be signed out once** on the first start of 4.0. Sign in again; later updates will not ask.

## Accounts and profiles
- **Nuvio accounts (experimental):** sign in by email or QR code. Every start asks **who is watching**, with your last profile in focus. Add profiles and change their name, colour and avatar on the TV.
- **Login:** a Stremio / Nuvio switch, the language as a list, and links to create an account.

## Interface
- **Side menu:** your profile at the top, **Leave the app** at the bottom.
- **Settings in two columns:** groups ordered by use, switches for on/off options, and **Sign out** at the end.
- **First start:** choose **Simple** or **Complete**, and the **Standard** or **Light** look (for webOS 3 and small TVs).
- **Home:** opens already filled, with **Because you watched…** and a top 10. Hide a series from **New episodes** with a long press on OK.
- **Latin American Spanish**, chosen by itself when the TV is in Spanish outside Spain.

## Player
- **New in its menu:** the season's episodes, chapters, other files of a torrent, picture size and speed.
- **Subtitles and audio:** your languages first, track details, subtitle position and opacity, finer delay, and optional ASS styling.
- A picture left paused for five minutes goes dark, to protect OLED screens.

## Storage and diagnostics
- Torrents can use a USB drive when the TV has no room, and **Settings** clears the torrent cache.
- **Test connection**, and **Diagnostics** showing the cache and how fast the remote is answered.
- After an update, the app says once what is new; if a start stopped early, it says at which step.

## Fixed
- The next episode starts even with slow addons, late episode lists or TVs that never report the end.
- Anime and long series (such as One Piece) show their name and picture in the player.
- Odd names in Continue Watching and New episodes ("tt1234567", "Show S08E01").
- Going back to a season returns to the episode it was left on; long Home descriptions roll instead of being cut.
- The library button shows its plus sign once.
- Trailers that cannot play here open in the TV's YouTube app.

Choose `StremCompat-v4.0.0-1080p.ipk` for Full HD and 4K screens, or `StremCompat-v4.0.0-720p.ipk` for smaller screens.
