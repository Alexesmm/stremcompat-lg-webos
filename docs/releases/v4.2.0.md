# StremCompat 4.2.0

## New
- **Skip intro and recap.** A button appears while an episode's opening or recap plays, and OK jumps past it. The times come from IntroDB (series) and AniSkip (anime), and from chapters named like an opening or a recap. Where the end credits begin, the next-episode card shows up.
- **Nuvio Collections on Home.** With a Nuvio account, the profile's Collections show as rows of folders, as in Nuvio's own apps; a folder opens its catalogs. Thanks to @shayanbm.
- **Updates from inside the app.** When a new version is out, an **Update** item appears in the side menu, above Leave. On LG it installs the new version through the Homebrew Channel and opens the app again.
- **Korean, Japanese and Simplified Chinese** as interface languages.
- **Support** in Settings, with a QR code for Buy Me a Coffee.

## Fixed
- **Newer TVs (webOS 10):** when the TV does not let the app run its local server as a separate process, it now runs inside the app's service. If the app still cannot start, the error screen says what the TV answered.

Everything new in 4.1 is in the [4.1.0 notes](https://github.com/Alexesmm/stremcompat-lg-webos/blob/main/docs/releases/v4.1.0.md).

Choose `StremCompat-v4.2.0-1080p.ipk` for Full HD and 4K screens, or `StremCompat-v4.2.0-720p.ipk` for smaller screens.
