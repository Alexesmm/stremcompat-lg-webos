# StremCompat 4.0.1

## New
- **Settings > History > Always keep torrents on the USB drive:** spares the TV's own storage. Without it, the drive is used only when the TV runs out of room.

## Fixed
- The update notice no longer mentions three looks.
- **Sign out** in Settings stands out in red, as intended.
- A Nuvio account shows as "Nuvio account" in Settings instead of "Free account".
- Clearer name for the option that brings back series removed from **New episodes**.

Updating from 4.0.0 keeps you signed in. Updating from 3.x signs you out once, as described in the [4.0.0 notes](v4.0.0.md).

Choose `StremCompat-v4.0.1-1080p.ipk` for Full HD and 4K screens, or `StremCompat-v4.0.1-720p.ipk` for smaller screens.
