# StremCompat 4.1.2

## New
- **Korean (한국어)** as an interface language, in **Settings > Interface > Interface language**. TVs set to Korean open in it. Korean is also in the audio languages of Best source.

## Fixed
- The names shown above the player buttons are centred on them.

Everything new in 4.1 is in the [4.1.0 notes](https://github.com/Alexesmm/stremcompat-lg-webos/blob/main/docs/releases/v4.1.0.md), and the fixes of 4.1.1 in its [notes](https://github.com/Alexesmm/stremcompat-lg-webos/blob/main/docs/releases/v4.1.1.md).

Choose `StremCompat-v4.1.2-1080p.ipk` for Full HD and 4K screens, or `StremCompat-v4.1.2-720p.ipk` for smaller screens.
