# StremCompat 3.0.0

The package goes from 46 MB to about 6 MB, one package now serves every LG TV, and torrents no longer depend on Stremio's server or on a bundled Node.js.

## Before you install

- **Coming from `StremCompat`:** this updates it.
- **Coming from `StremCompat HD`, `StremCompat UJ3` or `StremCompat UJ3 HD`:** remove that app. This package replaces all of them.
- You may need to sign in again. Your library, progress and addons are on your Stremio account.
- Right after updating, restart the TV once: a streaming server from the old version can keep running in the background until then.

## What changed

- **Much smaller:** about 6 MB to download and 13 MB installed, instead of 46 and 92.
- **One package:** the app chooses its limits by itself when it opens.
- **Lighter on memory:** the local server runs on the TV's own Node.js.
- **Torrents on the TV, from our own engine:** built on Nuvio Engine and libtorrent. It starts with the first torrent, stops when nothing plays, and clears what it kept on storage.
- **New look:** cleaner Home and title pages, more titles on screen, and round action buttons.
- **Best source:** one press starts the source that suits this TV and its connection.
- **Seven languages:** English, Brazilian Portuguese, Spanish, French, German, Italian and Russian.
- **Homebrew Channel:** install and update from our own repository, `https://alexesmm.github.io/stremcompat-lg-webos/apps.json`, and the app tells you when a new version is out.
- **Long pauses:** a torrent left paused for a long time opens again where it was.
- **Sharper art:** a title no longer stays on the blurry image when the app was busy.
- **Subtitles:** ASS and SSA files from addons are read by the app.
- **Licensing:** FFmpeg is an LGPL build made from source, with the exact options and checksums included.

## Good to know

- Torrents keep up to 768 MB of the film on storage while they play, depending on the free space.
- Trackers that only work over HTTPS are not used by the engine; UDP trackers and DHT are.
