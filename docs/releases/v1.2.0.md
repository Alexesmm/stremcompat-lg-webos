# StremCompat 1.2.0

This release makes everyday use faster on the remote, makes playback recover from bad sources, and keeps the lightweight runtime designed for older LG televisions. It is tested against the Chromium 38 engine used by webOS 3 and keeps the Chromium 53 target of webOS 4.

## Highlights

### Watching

- The title page starts with one-press actions: Continue S2 E5 · 32 min left, Play S1 E9, or Continue · 48 min left for a film.
- Continue Watching shows the episode and the minutes left, and a finished episode becomes its next episode.
- At the end of an episode the next one starts after a 10-second countdown. After three episodes in a row without a key press, the player asks whether anyone is still watching.
- The next episode's sources are requested during the end credits, so the next title page opens with them ready.
- When a source will not play, the player offers Try another source.
- The audio language, subtitle language and subtitle delay chosen for a series are kept for its next episodes.
- LG remote shortcuts in the player: red subtitles, green audio, yellow skips 85 seconds, blue settings, digits jump through the video.

### Finding something to watch

- Search has its own on-screen keyboard with results that appear while typing, and keeps the last searches one key away.
- Home adds New episodes of followed series and My list; catalog rows end with a See all card that opens Discover.
- Sources show short tags (quality, HDR/DV, codec, audio, size, seeders) and a recommended source for the television.
- Addons that do not answer are named instead of being reported as "no sources".

### Interface

- Inter typeface, a solid focus ring without blur, labels in the side menu while it has focus, and clearer empty screens.
- Settings are grouped by what is looked for first and add Interface text: Larger, History (clear recent searches or Continue Watching) and Diagnostics.

## Fixes

- Back returns to the previous title and card instead of losing it.
- Episodes with missing or repeated numbers no longer change order between openings on Chromium 38/53, and overall numbering no longer repeats or skips numbers.
- The player's next episode crosses into the next season and disappears at the series finale or before an unaired episode.
- Returning from playback no longer reopens the source list.
- The player control buttons are spaced correctly on Chromium 38/53 (flex `gap` is not supported there).
- After Back from Discover, the side menu points at the section on screen again.

## Performance

- The progress bar is only repainted while visible, the subtitle lookup no longer scans every cue on each update, and loading animations use opacity only.
- The source list draws addon answers together instead of rebuilding for each one.
- ffmpeg/ffprobe helpers run at a lower priority than playback, and the local server uses a smaller V8 young generation.
- The local server restarts on its own if it exits, and the launcher opens the interface as soon as the service is ready.
