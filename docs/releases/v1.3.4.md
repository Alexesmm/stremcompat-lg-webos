# StremCompat 1.3.4

## New

- The Home background now shows the whole image in the upper-right corner and fades smoothly into the dark background, without the hard cut-off edge. Images are also less enlarged and saved at higher quality, so they look sharper.
- Moving between titles now fades the background instead of switching it abruptly.
- Discover no longer shows a background behind the grid, which also saves memory.

## Fixes

- Home and Library no longer jump back to the top when the focus moves to the sidebar. Pressing Right returns to the same row, and choosing the section in the sidebar goes back to its start.
- In Discover, the highlight of a focused title in the first row is no longer cut off or touching the filters.
