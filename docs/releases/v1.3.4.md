# StremCompat 1.3.4

## New

- The Home background now shows the whole image in the upper-right corner and fades smoothly into the dark background, without the hard cut-off edge. Title pages get the same soft edge. Images are also less enlarged and saved at higher quality, so they look sharper.
- Moving between titles now fades the background instead of switching it abruptly.
- Discover no longer shows a background behind the grid, which also saves memory.
- The Home title area now shows runtime, year and an IMDb badge, plus the main cast. Title pages use the same info line.
- Information text across the app is now white or gray, with purple kept for highlights.

## Fixes

- Home and Library no longer jump back to the top when the focus moves to the sidebar. Pressing Right returns to the same row, and choosing the section in the sidebar goes back to its start.
- In Discover, the highlight of a focused title in the first row is no longer cut off or touching the filters.
- The next episode card, partly visible on screen, no longer stays black until the focus gets closer.
- The poster no longer sticks out above the source list.
- Text behind the open sidebar no longer shows through the section names.
