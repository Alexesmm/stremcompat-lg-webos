# StremCompat 1.3.2

## Fixes

- The seek interval is now exact. Pressing left or right jumps by the interval set in Settings (for example 10 s), and the on-screen indicator shows that amount. Before, the video kept playing while the presses added up, so a 10 s step often showed and jumped about 9 s.
- The seek bar keeps the same length while the time next to it counts.
- Sources that are `.rar`, `.zip` or `.7z` archives, which the TV cannot play, now go to the end of the source list with a warning and are never recommended. Release names such as "RARBG" are not affected.
