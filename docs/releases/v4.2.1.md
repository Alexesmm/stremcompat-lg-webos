# StremCompat 4.2.1

## Better
- **The language filter of the source list tells audio from subtitles much better**, including releases with many subtitle flags and Brazilian sources marked Dublado or Legendado.
- **The filter is simpler:** By audio (dubbed) or By embedded subtitles, then the language. Remove the filter appears only while one is on.

Everything new in 4.2 is in the [4.2.0 notes](https://github.com/Alexesmm/stremcompat-lg-webos/blob/main/docs/releases/v4.2.0.md).

Choose `StremCompat-v4.2.1-1080p.ipk` for Full HD and 4K screens, or `StremCompat-v4.2.1-720p.ipk` for smaller screens.
