# StremCompat 3.0.6

- **Torrents on a TV with little free space:** they no longer fill the TV's storage. With less than 150 MB free, torrents do not start and the app says so. TVs with enough space keep the same cache as before. Thanks to [@Biditmangal](https://github.com/Biditmangal) (#9).
- **Debrid links in the source list:** they no longer disappear when a torrent addon fills the list. Based on #8 by [@Biditmangal](https://github.com/Biditmangal).
- **Addons:** one that fails with a server error, or does not answer, is asked once more.
- **4K badge:** films in a cinema format, such as 3840x1600, are shown as 4K on the paused screen instead of 1080p.
- **Diagnostics:** free space is read reliably on more TVs.

Choose `StremCompat-v3.0.6-1080p.ipk` for Full HD and 4K screens, or `StremCompat-v3.0.6-720p.ipk` for smaller screens.
