# StremCompat 1.2.1

This patch release fixes Continue Watching synchronization with other Stremio apps and makes the player get out of the way while watching.

## Fixes

- Progress watched on the television now reaches the account reliably: leaving the player waits for the last progress push instead of changing page while it is in flight.
- Titles already on the television are updated again with progress made on other devices. Modification times from the account were compared as text, so existing items were never refreshed.
- At start-up, progress that is newer on the television than on the account, or missing there, is sent again.
- A title removed from the library and watched again now appears in Continue Watching on other Stremio apps.

## Player

- After five seconds without input (eight inside the settings menu), the controls and menus close and only the video stays; the next key brings them back.
