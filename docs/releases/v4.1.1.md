# StremCompat 4.1.1

## Fixed
- **Titles in your language:** with a metadata addon such as **TMDB** set to your language, title pages show the name, synopsis and episode titles in it, also for titles from Cinemeta catalogs and Continue watching. Before, they stayed in English.
- **The source list** is ordered by compatibility and seeders again. The audio language chosen in Best source applies only to Best source; to order the whole list by language, set it in **Settings > Playback > Audio language of sources**.
- A dubbed torrent with almost no seeders no longer goes ahead of the healthy sources.

Everything new in 4.1 is in the [4.1.0 notes](https://github.com/Alexesmm/stremcompat-lg-webos/blob/main/docs/releases/v4.1.0.md).

Choose `StremCompat-v4.1.1-1080p.ipk` for Full HD and 4K screens, or `StremCompat-v4.1.1-720p.ipk` for smaller screens.
