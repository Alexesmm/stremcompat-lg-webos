# StremCompat 4.2.2

## New
- **Mark as watched or unwatched anywhere.** Hold OK on a film to mark it, on an episode for just that episode, or on the season button for the whole season. It syncs with your account like the official apps. Thanks to @automatafinito.
- **Air dates on episodes**, including those still to come. They can be turned off in **Settings > Interface**.

## Fixed
- The notice after an update now just names the new version.

Choose `StremCompat-v4.2.2-1080p.ipk` for Full HD and 4K screens, or `StremCompat-v4.2.2-720p.ipk` for smaller screens.
