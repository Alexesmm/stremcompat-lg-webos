# StremCompat 1.3.0

## New

- Watched state now syncs both ways like the official Stremio apps. Episodes and films watched on the TV are marked watched on your phone and computer, and finishing an episode moves Continue Watching to the next one on every device. Episodes finished on another device now show up as the next episode on the TV too.
- Sources show their audio language (for example `PT-BR`, `ES-LA`, `EN` or `MULTI`), read from the addon's flags and release names.
- New **Settings > Sources > Preferred source language**: sources in that language are listed first.
- When the sources come in more than one language, a small language selector in the corner of the source list filters them.
- When the TV cannot reach Stremio, the login and Home screens now say so and suggest checking the connection or turning off IPv6, instead of only showing a generic error.
- Redesigned startup screen, matching the rest of the interface, in Portuguese or English according to the TV language.

## Fixes

- The title no longer appears twice for a moment when pausing a video.
- A title counts as finished past 90 % of it, the same rule as the official apps.
